Our Story

Twin Cities Animal Rescue is a community nonprofit built around a shared purpose: helping animals reach safe, caring homes. Our story is one of neighbors making room in their lives for animals who need a new beginning.

Volunteers, foster caregivers, adopters, and donors each help move that mission forward. We connect people with practical ways to care for animals and strengthen our community.

Our Core Values

  • Compassion: treat animals and people with patience and respect.
  • Responsible care: consider each animal's needs in every placement.
  • Trust: communicate clearly about care and expectations.
  • Community: welcome people with different skills and experiences.

Thank You to Our Partners

We appreciate the veterinary teams, local businesses, community organizations, and generous neighbors who support animal care, supplies, and outreach.
